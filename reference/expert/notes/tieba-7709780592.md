## Strong agreements with the 209-streak guide

1. **烧牌不是一个静态标签。** 旧文把它写成“当回合燃料/费用代价、即时补偿、未来循环收益”的矛盾；新文把它写成启动、转换和终端卡组。两种语言指向同一个工程要求：必须评价轨迹，而不是数 Exhaust 牌。
2. **Second Wind 有很低的条件下限。** 旧文虽给出极高评分，却明确称其组合难度最大、受非攻击燃料和手牌事故制约；新文进一步强调手牌量与可烧目标密度。二者共同反对空放重振或仅凭卡名认定引擎成立。
3. **时期决定边际价值。** 旧文的过渡牌章节区分前期赚血、二层适应和后期循环负担；新文区分即时下限与可达上限。两者都不支持跨时期固定分。
4. **卡组访问能力和费用必须与数值联合看。** 旧文用卡位、升级位、过牌与费用解释集中/分散的取舍；新文用启动、访问和端口缺口解释。同一张牌可能提高当前稳定性，也可能增加未来处理负担。
5. **Flame Barrier 不是远期 Boss 标签。** 旧文认为反伤对三鸟/杀人书只是添头，主要价值是集中卡位的防御；新文也把多段对策限定在具体遭遇与机会成本中。

## Direct tensions and why they matter

### “通解一定是核卡组” is too strong

Floors 12–16 first exclude potion-, luck- and some relic-dependent wins as “special solutions”, then infer that the general solution must be a small repeatable core. This is a useful idealized construction, not a production invariant. It deletes legal run resources before proving universality and conflicts with the later guide's continuum between concentrated and distributed production.

Engineering consequence: record where value production is concentrated and how it is accessed; never require every winning deck to converge to a predeclared core form.

### Combat loss is evidence, not the whole objective

Floor 61 insightfully describes HP as something traded for upgrades, elites, events and future strength. Floor 64 then slides toward “every combat should minimize loss”. The newer guide's option-budget model exposes the missing term: using a potion, delaying setup, preserving Feed/Reaper windows, or changing route readiness can make a locally lower-loss line worse for the run.

Engineering consequence: terminal combat HP belongs in a resource vector with consumables and resulting state; it cannot become a lexicographic zero-loss command.

### ZERO-MAX is a provocative heuristic, not literal efficiency

Floor 70 calls zero-cost cards infinitely fee-efficient. That ratio hides hand slots, draw/access cost, Time Warp, statuses, duplication, target availability and whether the zero-cost effect advances the encounter. The useful remainder is simply that energy pressure can sometimes be solved by reducing the cost of *needed actions*, not only by adding energy.

Engineering consequence: represent energy feasibility and action/access burden directly; do not encode infinity or a blanket zero-cost bonus.

### Score tables carry their own warning label

Floor 95 says scores depend on player style, SL scope, phase, current deck and conflicts between similar functions. The detailed catalog is therefore excellent for roles and counterexamples, but its numeric order cannot be a teacher label.

## New reusable dimensions

- **Card-slot concentration:** one high-impact card can save reward slots, upgrades, draw and later exhaust/removal work.
- **Card-slot distribution:** several smaller cards can improve draw-order robustness, flexibility, strength scaling and resistance to statuses.
- **Immediate compensation for setup:** an engine-enabling action may also block, deal damage, draw or refund energy; this changes whether setup survives.
- **Encounter-specific mode:** “攻杀/防杀” is a description of how a concrete deck reduces loss in a concrete fight, not a permanent deck archetype.
- **Reload-policy provenance:** advice learned under SL may be useful for Node-Reload but cannot silently label No-SL value.

## Project implications

The strategic review surface should eventually expose, for each reward candidate and Skip:

- immediate numerical contribution and encounter coverage;
- reachable engine contribution and its prerequisites;
- draw/access, energy and hand burden;
- upgrade, removal and exhaust burden;
- duplication or target-density conflicts;
- resulting HP, potion and route-option changes;
- evidence provenance, including SL/No-SL scope and game version.

These are review fields and hypothesis generators. They do not prescribe a weighted sum, fixed score, forced card, or production action.
